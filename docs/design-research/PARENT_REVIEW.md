# UpgradeAtlas design research — parent review

This supersedes the two preliminary delegated audits for decisions. No destination app code, database, dependency installation, purchase or publishing was performed in this research increment. All 18 actual `Ai / DESIGN` bookmark entries were inventoried, split into 11 visual/gallery references and 7 component entries; not all search-derived access claims were independently confirmed. The bookmark export omits credential-bearing query strings and leaves the originals untouched.

## Accepted visual observations

- **Haptikos:** the captured live viewport presents a large monochrome physical device/hand visual with sparse navigation. Borrow the hardware focal point and material lighting; do not borrow its brand, video, cookie overlay or low-contrast monochrome treatment.[3]
- **Exact Agility Digit 5 bookmark:** the actual Refero page presents an embedded source preview and a neutral engineering/industrial style description. The child audited a different ID; its yellow/robot.com palette is not the saved reference and is rejected. Borrow engineering imagery and disciplined technical grouping, not another brand's hero.[5]
- **monopo saigon:** Refero shows a source thumbnail with liquid/iridescent imagery and an editorial layout description. Borrow the distinction between expressive imagery and restrained interface; do not introduce decorative molten blobs into laptop browsing.[4]
- **Apple comparison:** parent inspected consistent model selectors above aligned product images with clear whitespace. Borrow consistent comparison geometry and product focus, not Apple data, imagery, fonts or layout wholesale.[1]

Godly was successfully viewed, but several thumbnails did not load; it remains a discovery index, not a verified collection of selected hardware examples.[2] Framework returned 408 in the direct capture pass, so no visual conclusions are accepted from that viewport. Unlumen's captured viewport was largely empty below navigation; the successful HTTP response does not establish that the component previews loaded. Originkit's captured homepage contained several blank animation tiles; unseen effects were not visually verified.

## Proposed direction for confirmation

**Material clarity:** a light/silver interface, strong Arabic/Latin typography, deep ink text, one cobalt action accent, and hardware imagery doing the expressive work. Dedicated dark mode can follow the same hierarchy. A dramatic device image is compatible with a calm light interface; do not alternate whole-page themes or build a dark developer dashboard.

The intended experience remains the full product: home with buying/ownership entry points, useful finder/catalog, exact-SKU detail, 2–4-product comparison and evidence-aware upgrade advice. The calculator becomes a supporting owner tool. Search, evidence and comparisons take precedence over ornamental animation. Product images need their own permitted asset source; reference-site screenshots are private inspiration, not reusable listing photos.

Proposed dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 6 (distinctive but learnable), MOTION_INTENSITY 3 (feedback, not spectacle), VISUAL_DENSITY 4 for discovery and higher only where comparison information warrants it. These are proposals, not claims of completed implementation.

`reference-direction.png` is a private reference collage, not the new website. `MATERIAL_CLARITY.md` records its visual philosophy. The remaining grilling decision is the primary presentation: light/silver (recommended) versus graphite/dark; do not silently choose the opposite of the user's intent.
