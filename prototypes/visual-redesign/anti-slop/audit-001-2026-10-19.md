## HIGH — Hard Gate

### 1. R-02 — Em dash in shipped UI copy (3 rendered strings + the document title)
- `src/prototype-home.jsx:24` — "Unknown information should stay visible**—**not become a confident-looking specification."
- `src/prototype-catalog.jsx:41` — "All products, specifications and prices below are illustrative**—**not verified offers."
- `src/prototype-catalog.jsx:12` — "These filters act on fictional design samples**—**not a real laptop database." (renders twice: desktop rail + mobile filter dialog)
- `src/prototype.jsx:20` — `document.title` = `"Overview **—** UpgradeAtlas design study"` on all three pages, both locales.

Reason: R-02 forbids the em dash character in any text the agent writes; a comma, period, colon or parenthesis carries the same meaning without the banned glyph. Verified by a text-node walk of 3 pages × 2 locales and by grep over `src/`.

### 2. R-03 / R-35 — Activating the 3D inspector re-flows the hero and clips the headline
`.hero` is a scroll container (`overflow:hidden`) whose absolutely positioned child `.hero-visual` bleeds 46px past the right edge, so `scrollWidth 1375 > clientWidth 1329`. When the WebGL canvas mounts, the browser scrolls that container:

| State | `hero.scrollLeft` | `.hero h1` left edge |
|---|---|---|
| before Inspect (EN) | 0 | 48px |
| after Inspect (EN) | **+30.4px** | **18px** |
| after Inspect (AR) | **−29.6px** | mirrored, off the right edge |

Result: the headline's first glyph is cut by the viewport edge in EN, and the copy is pushed off the right edge in AR. `.hero-copy`, `.hero h1`, `.hero-lead`, `.hero .button.primary` and `.hero-visual` all move together; the hero box itself does not. The 3D control cluster also geometrically overlaps the honesty caption, hiding "Original concept study. Not a retail model." (controls 1132–1376 × 698–742; caption 1167–1367 × 732–750).

Reason: R-03 requires the mobile/desktop layout to hold in every state, and R-35 requires the claim to be backed by the run rather than by code reading. A user-initiated control must not change the page's composition.

Fix direction: `overflow:clip` on `.hero` (clip does not create a scroll container, so no `scrollLeft` can move), and lift `.studio-controls` clear of the caption.

### 3. R-25 — Adjacent chart segments fall below the 3:1 non-text contrast bar
Measured on the live composition bar (`.composition-bar`, track `#242b35`):

| Pair | Ratio | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| part0 `#c1c7cf` vs part1 `#646f80` | **2.99** | 3:1 |
| part1 `#646f80` vs part2 `#e5484d` | **1.30** | 3:1 |
| part1 `#646f80` vs track | **2.80** | 3:1 |

Reason: the segments are the only way to read the upgrade composition, so their boundaries are meaningful graphical objects and must be distinguishable (R-25; antislop-ui "chart segments still need 3:1 contrast against their neighbours"). The middle segment is currently 1.3:1 from its neighbour.

### 4. R-31 / C-1 — The hero's hard-edged backdrop plane reads as a render failure
`research/art-direction.md` specifies "one quiet grounded studio platform" for the homepage. The delivered poster instead terminates the studio floor in a hard diagonal, and at the desktop crop that diagonal becomes a large black wedge across the lower-left of the hero, touching the headline. It reads as an unrendered asset rather than a chosen shape, and the same hard band repeats across the top of all six catalog card images.

Reason: R-31 requires every major decision to pass the one-line reason test, and C-1 requires intentionality; an artefact that reads as an error has no articulable reason, and it contradicts the art direction that specified a *quiet* platform.

Evidence: `home-en-desktop.png`, `home-ar-desktop.png`, `home-en-tablet.png`, `catalog-en-desktop.png` in the capture set. The fix belongs in the 3D scene (`motion/studio.js` / `motion/render.py`), not in CSS.

## MEDIUM — Purpose-Gate

### 5. R-08 — The arrow glyph is on every button-like control
7 of the 7 matched controls on the home page carry the arrow: "Explore laptops", "Already own a laptop? Compare upgrade costs", "Search laptops", "Open the cost workbench", and all three mobile-nav rows. Catalog "Compare concepts" and calculator "Calculate comparison" repeat it. 7 of 20 controls on the page.

Reason: R-08 says arrows are not the default identity for every button; once every CTA has one, the arrow stops being a direction cue and becomes wallpaper.

### 6. R-01 / R-31 — The brand red does two jobs
`research/art-direction.md` states the rule the design already committed to: *"Red #E5484D is decision/action, not every title."* The calculator's composition bar uses `--red` as the **Installation data series**, so a category colour reads as an action or an error. The same red also drives the notice border, the selected compare button, the active nav underline, the three principle line-symbols and the brand mark.

Reason: R-31 — a colour used as both action and category cannot be written down as one reason, so the decision fails the keystone rule and the palette loses its hierarchy.

### 7. R-05 / C-3 — The interior page intro leaves a dead quadrant
`.page-intro` is `grid-template-columns:1fr .83fr` with `align-items:end`, so on the catalog and calculator the short right-hand column bottom-aligns under roughly 160px of empty space while the left column is dense. With the 100px column gap, the top-right of both interior pages is void.

Reason: R-05 and C-3 — whitespace should structure the page, and here it reads as a layout that was not composed rather than as deliberate space.

### 8. R-09 — "Concept" is stated three times per screen
A "Concept" tag on each of the six product cards, "Concept visuals · example data" in the result line, and the page-level disclaimer. The per-card tag repeats a fact the section already carries.

Reason: R-09 — badges are for a functional need, and the function is already met twice per screen; the repetition is noise, not information.

### 9. R-31 / R-21 — The theme decision is not recorded where it is read
Dark-only is well founded: `research/art-direction.md` grounds it in two saved reference systems and in the dark hardware photography the page is built around. But `DESIGN.md` — the document the implementation is held to — states the palette without stating *why* dark is the fixed theme, and nothing states that a light mode was considered and rejected.

Reason: R-31 asks for a one-line reason per major decision; R-21 asks that a fixed theme be a decision. Recording the reason in `DESIGN.md` satisfies both without changing the design.

## LOW — Quality Locks

### 10. R-03 — The brand link's hit area is under the 44px minimum
`.brand` measures 156 × 25px in the header and 120 × 30px in the footer. All 45 other controls on the three pages measure ≥ 44px.

### 11. R-32 — The compare table's "rows that differ" cue is colour-only
`.compare-table .different td { background:#25191d }` against `#111419`. The row logic itself is **correct** (verified: Storage 512 GB / 512 GB is not tinted; Memory, Screen and Illustrative cost are), but a reader who cannot separate those two maroons gets no redundant signal.

### 12. R-38 — The brand mark is an unapproved invented asset
The two-stroke "//" glyph is a concept identity with no approval record; `DESIGN.md` treats it as identity. No fabricated people, testimonials, statistics, stock indicators or performance claims exist anywhere in the prototype — that part of R-38 is clean. The glyph is the only asset that outruns its evidence.

## Rules to lock (add to `DESIGN.md`)

Each correction is a rule, not a one-off fix, so it holds for every future surface.

1. **No em dash in any shipped string.** Use a comma, period, colon or parenthesis. Applies to body copy, eyebrows, captions, button labels, `alt` text, `document.title` and the Arabic strings.
2. **The hero is a clipping box, never a scroll container.** `overflow:clip` on `.hero`; no element inside it may ever be able to scroll the container. Any overlay that mounts over the hero must not move a single hero pixel.
3. **The data ramp is not the action colour.** `--red` is reserved for actions and states. Charts and categorical series use a neutral three-step ramp; categorical boundaries clear 3:1 against their neighbour and against the track.
4. **One directional arrow per screen, on the primary forward action.** Search, filter, compare-tray and navigation rows carry no arrow.
5. **Overlay controls never sit on honesty copy.** The caption that states the visual is not a product shot owns its own band; controls sit above or beside it, never across it.
6. **The studio platform stays quiet.** The floor/backdrop edge must not terminate inside the frame as a hard diagonal. If the platform is meant to be seen, its boundary is designed; if not, it falls off frame.
7. **Every tap target is at least 44 × 44px**, including text links in the header and footer.
8. **A colour-only state cue gets a redundant cue.** A tinted row also changes weight or gains a marker.
